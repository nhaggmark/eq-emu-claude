## Section 1 — VP Key Quest (Key of Veeshan)

**Quest name:** Key of Veeshan (Rune of Scale)
**Quest-giver NPC:** Emperor Ganak, Trakanon's Teeth
**Purpose:** Unlocks Veeshan's Peak — required before any VP dragon kill counts for epic progression.
**Era:** Kunark

### Step-by-step

**Sub-quest A: Medallion of Jarsath**
- Piece 1: Ground spawn (Swamp of No Hope, 8-hour respawn, loc varies)
- Piece 2: An Ancient Jarsath — named sarnak, Firiona Vie (outdoor spawn)
- Piece 3: A Bloodgill Marauder — froglok, Lake of Ill Omen (underwater area, 8-hour respawn)
- Combine 3 pieces → Medallion of Jarsath
- Pain score: YELLOW (underwater camp + rare spawn timing, no raid required)

**Sub-quest B: Medallion of Obulus**
- Piece 1: Burnished Wooden Staff quest in Warsliks Woods (requires delivering Iksar faction — non-Iksar races start at hostile; faction grind ~6 hrs OR illusion item required)
- Piece 2: Rotting Skeleton — rare spawn, Dreadlands (~6-hour window)
- Piece 3: Pained Soul — rare spawn, Trakanon's Teeth
- Combine 3 pieces → Medallion of Obulus
- Pain score: RED for non-Iksar (mandatory faction grind blocks this sub-quest cold without Iksar illusion or faction grind; rare spawns add to timeline)

**Sub-quest C: Medallion of Kylong**
- Piece 1: Niblek's Gems sub-quest, Chardok (give gems to Niblek → Gem Encrusted Ring → give to Queen's courier → Medallion Piece)
- Piece 2: Verix Kylox's Remains — named skeleton, Karnor's Castle basement (~8-hour respawn)
- Piece 3: Ground spawn in Kaesora library (~8-hour respawn)
- Combine 3 pieces → Medallion of Kylong
- Pain score: YELLOW (Chardok navigation, rare KC camp, but no raid required)

**Final step:**
- Give Medallion of Jarsath + Medallion of Obulus + Medallion of Kylong + Trakanon's Tooth to Emperor Ganak
- Trakanon's Tooth drops from Trakanon (standard or triggered). Trakanon is full raid.
- Receive: Key of Veeshan
- Pain score: RED (Trakanon is a raid boss; standard Trakanon ~200k HP with AE poison breath that wipes groups; death touch component in some versions)

**VP Key Summary:**
| Step | Zone | Raid Required? | Pain Score |
|------|------|---------------|------------|
| Medallion of Jarsath | Swamp of No Hope, FV, LakIO | No | YELLOW |
| Medallion of Obulus | Warsliks, Dreadlands, TT | No (faction issue) | RED (non-Iksar) |
| Medallion of Kylong | Chardok, Karnor's, Kaesora | No | YELLOW |
| Trakanon's Tooth | Old Sebilis | YES — Trakanon RAID BOSS | RED |
| Final Key | Trakanon's Teeth | No (Emperor Ganak is quest NPC) | — |

## Section 2 — Old Sebilis Key (Trakanon Idol)

**Quest name:** Trakanon Idol
**Quest-giver NPC:** Emperor Ganak, Trakanon's Teeth
**Purpose:** Unlocks Old Sebilis zone — required for Trakanon access and several epic steps (Bard, Necromancer[partial], Wizard[Gnarled Staff from VS variant])
**Era:** Kunark

### Step-by-step
- Kill froglok forager (Lake of Ill Omen) → loot Medallion of Nathsar (body drop, uncommon)
- Kill froglok hunter (Lake of Ill Omen) → loot Medallion of Kunzar (body drop, uncommon)
- Give both medallions to Emperor Ganak → receive Trakanon Idol
- Pain score: GREEN (no raid required, both mobs are regular named frogloks in outdoor zone, can multi-quest)

**Sebilis Key Summary:** Cleanest Kunark key — two zone-camping sessions, no raid encounter, no faction gate. The gating challenge is just respawn timing.

## Section 3 — "Skyfire Hand Quest" (Magician Epic Step, Not a Standalone Chain)

The task brief references a "Skyfire hand quest." Research confirms this is not an independent quest chain. It refers to a specific step within the Magician Epic 1.0 quest:

- **NPC:** Jennus Lyklobar — roaming gnome, Skyfire Mountains (level 61, sees invis, tends to path into solid terrain — tracker required to locate)
- **Items required:** Blazing Wand + Burning Embers + Torch of the Elements + Power of the Orb
- **Reward:** Element of Fire
- **Context:** These four items are each the culmination of separate sub-quests across Classic and Kunark zones. Jennus assembles them into the Element of Fire, which combines with the other three Elemental Staves to form the Orb of Mastery.
- **Raid encounters for the four sub-items:** Magi P`Tasa (Plane of Hate — raid), Phinigel Autropos (Kedge Keep — elite named, soloable with care), Captain Rottgrime (Overthere — group mob), Tarbul Earthstrider (East Karana — group mob). None of the pre-Jennus items require a kill in Skyfire Mountains itself.
- **Jennus himself:** Quest NPC, not a combat encounter.
- Pain score for Jennus step: GREEN (turn-in only, but preceded by Plane of Hate raid for Magi P`Tasa)

### 4.8 Paladin (Fiery Avenger)
**Kunark zones:** City of Mist (Kilidna), multiple

| Step | Zone | NPC / Mob | Action | Raid? | Pain |
|------|------|-----------|--------|-------|------|
| Ghoulbane + combine steps | Various Classic zones | Various | Collect + combine 4 items | No | GREEN–YELLOW |
| Culminating combat: Lhranc | City of Mist | Lhranc (L57, ~35k HP) | Give 4 combined items; human form spawns and attacks; kill human form; Marl Kastane spawns | Not full raid — 1–2 groups | YELLOW |

**Lore note:** Lhranc is the final combat encounter for BOTH Paladin AND Shadow Knight epics. He is the spirit of a Shadow Knight lord trapped in City of Mist. For Paladins, Lhranc (undead form at loc 0,90) must receive the four components; human Lhranc spawns and attacks; once killed, Heart of the Innocent and Lhranc's Token can be obtained. For Shadow Knights, Lhranc receives the Corrupted Ghoulbane and other items to produce Lhranc's Token. Different items, same NPC, different outcomes. If Lhranc needs to be killed multiple times (for Paladin and SK epics in the same small group), this is doable.

Kilidna note: From game-designer's Kunark boss catalog — Kilidna (City of Mist boss) is flagged at max damage 4,600. This is one-shot range for any tank below ~5k HP. If the small group visits City of Mist for the Paladin epic, they may encounter Kilidna on the roaming path to Lhranc. Kilidna should not be on the required kill list for the Paladin epic (Lhranc is the target), but zone navigation risk is real.

**Overall Kunark pain:** YELLOW — Lhranc is manageable but City of Mist pathing to him risks Kilidna proximity.

## Section 7 — Lore Flags for Audit

**Flag 1: Triggered VS vs. Standard VS — two NPC IDs**
The standard Venril Sathir (NPC ID ~102112) drops the Gnarled Staff for the Wizard epic. The triggered Venril Sathir (spawned from VS Remains via Firefly Globe + Rez scroll) is a different NPC and drops Paw of Opolla (Druid) and Spiroc Feather (Ranger). Both share the same lore identity but are mechanically distinct NPCs. The audit needs to track both NPC IDs for scaling.

**Flag 2: Triggered Trakanon vs. Standard Trakanon — two NPC IDs**
Standard Trakanon drops Trakanon's Tooth (VP key component). Triggered Trakanon (spawned via An Undead Bard in Old Sebilis) drops Undead Dragongut Strings (Bard epic). Again, same lore entity, distinct NPCs. Both need independent scaling treatment.

**Flag 3: The Tangrin is absent from the Kunark boss catalog**
The Tangrin (Field of Bone, L54, ~32k HP) is required for the Enchanter epic but was not in the initial Kunark boss catalog sent in task #3/#4. Add as ELITE NAMED in the Kunark section.

**Flag 4: Truespirit faction is a hard gate for 3 classes (Druid, Ranger, Shaman)**
No amount of raid scaling fixes this. The Truespirit chain is linear, non-repeatable, and NPC-dialogue-dependent. If the server's quest scripts have any dialogue trigger bugs, these epics brick. The audit should note this as an out-of-scope blocker for the scaling pass itself.

**Flag 5: Necromancer's PoSky step destroys shared raid resource**
Giving Tome of Instruction to Thunder Spirit Princess (PoSky Island 1) for the Necromancer Cloak of Spiroc Feathers quest despawns the Princess, preventing the Island 1 raid boss from spawning in that instance. This is a shared-server concern: on a multi-player server, one player's necro epic would break PoSky for everyone else in the same instance.
