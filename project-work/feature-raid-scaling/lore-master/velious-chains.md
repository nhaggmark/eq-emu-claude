## Key Lore Context: The Three Velious Factions

Velious content is organized around three major faction blocs that are mutually incompatible. A character can ally with at most two:

| Faction | Zone(s) | Allies |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Coldain Dwarves | Thurgadin, Eastern Wastes, Great Divide | Oppose Kromzek giants |
| Kromzek / Kael Giants | Kael Drakkel | Oppose Coldain; neutral on dragons |
| Claws of Veeshan (Dragons) | Skyshrine, Western Wastes, Temple of Veeshan | Oppose Coldain raids; neutral on some classes |

**Critical constraint for small-group server:** The player cannot ally with all three simultaneously. The Coldain Ring quest chain (which culminates in Ring 10 requiring the ring in the Icewell Keep) conflicts with Coldain faction needed for Thurgadin armor quests. For a single-character server building companions of different races/classes, faction management is a real constraint.

## Section 1 — Coldain Ring Quest Chain (Rings 1–10)

**Quest-giver:** Garadain Glacierbane (Eastern Wastes)
**Purpose:** Each ring is a reward item with increasing stats; Ring 10 (Ring of Dain Frostreaver IV) is one of the best pre-ToV rings in the game.
**Era:** Velious
**Total quests:** 10 sequential, must complete in order

### Ring-by-Ring Summary

| Ring | Name | Key Requirement | Raid? | Pain Score |
|------|------|-----------------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Copper Coldain Insignia Ring | Tailoring craft (trivial 41) — 2 HQ Cougar Skins + 2 HQ Tundra Kodiak Pelts | No | GREEN |
| 2 | Silver Coldain Insignia Ring | Escort Boridain Glacierbane; kill Rabid Tundra Kodiak; loot its skin | No — escort event | GREEN |
| 3 | Gold Coldain Insignia Ring | Gather Skinning Rock + Wooly Rhino Horn + HQ Walrus Hide; smith into Coldain Hunting Knife (BS trivial 41) | No | GREEN |
| 4 | Platinum Coldain Insignia Ring | Save Tain Hammerfrost from Ghrek Squatnot and frost giant ambush; loot Frozen Elixir | No — small group named | GREEN–YELLOW |
| 5 | Obsidian Coldain Insignia Ring | Kill Ry'Gorr Messenger; hand plans to lookout; trigger orc invasion; kill Scarbrow Ga'Hruk in battle | No — triggered event | YELLOW |
| 6 | Mithril Coldain Insignia Ring | Night escort with Korrigain and wolf Icefang; kill Poxbreath Yellowfang in orc camp with adds | No — multi-add fight | YELLOW |
| 7 | Adamantium Coldain Insignia Ring | Kill Warden Bruke; rescue and escort Corbin Blackwell through giant ambush; deliver note to correct Dobbin Crossaxe (black-bearded variant) | No — complex escort | YELLOW |

| 9 | Coldain Hero's Insignia Ring | Collect 5 traitor heads + Dirk of the Traitor; multiple instances in Kael Drakkel, Icewell Keep, Eastern Wastes, Great Divide — 10-minute timer on Juliash | Near-raid — multiple concurrent engagements | YELLOW–RED |
| 10 | Ring of Dain Frostreaver IV | Ring 10 War Event (see below) | YES — full raid | RED |

### Ring 8 Special Note
Ring 8 is the chain's first critical failure point. If the war event fails (Chief Ry'Gorr not killed within 4 minutes), the player loses access rights and must restart the entire chain from Ring 1. On a standard server, access rights are granted via a server-wide roll system — only one guild can hold the event at a time. On a single-player private server, this system may behave differently, but the 4-minute timer still applies.

### Ring 9 — The Traitor Hunt
Five traitors must be killed. Two are especially difficult:
- **Peffin Ambersnow** (Giant fort in Eastern Wastes): invisible, surrounded by five perma-rooted summoning giant guards. Ranged characters or careful tactics required.
- **Doldigun Steinwielder** (Kael Drakkel throne room): requires entering the most dangerous part of Kael, near King Tormax.
- **Councilor Juliash Lockheart** (Icewell Keep): 10-minute hard timer from spawn to kill. She despawns if not killed in time.

All five heads + the Dirk of the Traitor must be combined in the Traitor's Bane Box and delivered to Dain Frostreaver IV.

### Ring 10 — The War Event
**Starting sequence:**
1. Give Ring 9 + Declaration of War to Sentry Badain in Great Divide tower
2. Receive Orders of Engagement from Seneschal Aldikar
3. Give Orders of Engagement to Zrelik the Scout → triggers battle (30-minute clock starts)
4. Direct dwarf commanders using in-game say commands

**Wave structure:** 3 rounds × 7 waves each = 21 waves before the final boss
- Round 1: Kromrif Spearmen, Captains, Recruits, Clerics of Zek
- Round 2: Kromrif Generals, Warriors, Priests of Zek
- Round 3: Kromrif Warlords, Veterans, High Priests of Zek

**Final boss: Narandi the Wretched**
- Spawns after all 21 waves complete
- Described as "fairly simple tank and spank" relative to the event
- CRITICAL: Must keep dwarf NPC armies away from Narandi — they will die to him and failure cascades
- Kill Narandi; loot Narandi's Head; deliver to Seneschal Aldikar → receive Ring of Dain Frostreaver IV

**Event duration:** ~1 hour 45 minutes total
**Failure condition:** Waves spawn faster than they can be killed if raid is too small → cascading failure

**Pain score:** RED — this is a full server raid event. Cannot be completed with 1 player + 5 companions at standard tuning. The wave count alone requires sustained DPS that exceeds companion capability without significant boss-scaling intervention on wave mobs.

**Small-group assessment:** The Ring 10 event is the most structurally difficult Velious quest chain for a small group. It is not a "hard boss" problem — it is a sustained DPS-over-time problem across 21+ waves before the boss even spawns. Scaling only the named bosses (Narandi) does not solve this; the waves themselves gate the encounter.

## Section 2 — Kael Drakkel Raid Progression

**Zone access:** No key required. Enter from Eastern Wastes at coordinates -6300, +5000. Zone is level-appropriate open world.
**Faction required for vendors/quests:** Kindly+ Kromzek. Dubious+ allows head turn-ins.

### The Avatar of War Spawn Chain

The most prestigious Kael kill chain follows a strict progression:

| Step | Mob | Location | Notes |
|------|-----|----------|-------|
| 1 | The Statue of Rallos Zek | Kael Arena | Kills spawns The Idol of Rallos Zek |
| 2 | The Idol of Rallos Zek | Kael Arena | Kills spawns The Avatar of War |
| 3 | The Avatar of War | Kael Arena | ~1.7 million HP, hits 1,700+, flurries, fire immune |

**Avatar of War loot:** Blade of Carnage, Flayed Barbarian Hide Mask/Leggings, Helmet of Rallos Zek, Ring of Destruction, War Bow of Rallos Zek.

**Pain score:** RED — Avatar of War at 1.7M HP is the hardest fight in Kael. Well beyond companion group capability at default stats.

### King Tormax
**Location:** Throne room, Kael Drakkel
**Loot:** Gauntlets of Dragon Slaying (from Yelinak's Head turn-in quest), plus Tormax's own armor drops
**Quest interaction:** King Tormax head can be used as a faction/quest item in some chains. Also, killing King Tormax is one of three methods to obtain a Temple of Veeshan access key (confirmed by multiple sources — the key drops from King Tormax on death).
**Pain score:** RED — raid tier boss

### Derakor the Vindicator
**Location:** Temple of Rallos Zek area, Kael
**Loot:** Boots of the Vindicator, Chestplate of Vindication, Living Thunder Earring; also can drop a Shard of Hsagra's Talisman (one piece of the Sleeper's Tomb key pathway)
**Pain score:** RED — raid tier elite

### Thurgadin Armor Quest Chain (Coldain Faction Path)
**Faction required:** Ally with Coldain
**Mold source:** Kael Arena and King Tormax areas (drop from trash/named in these areas)
**Gem requirements:** Vary by class and armor slot
**Reward:** Full set of Thurgadin-branded plate/chain/cloth armor
**Pain score:** YELLOW overall — molds require running Kael arena trash repeatedly; King Tormax area molds require deeper Kael access. No individual "raid boss required" step, but the King Tormax area molds require surviving near raid-tier mobs.

## Section 3 — Temple of Veeshan Access and Quest Chains

### Zone Access
**Entry:** Clickable tower entrance in Western Wastes at +700, +700 (behind Sontalak spawn). Level 46+ required. **No key needed for entry.**

**Internal faction gating:**
- West Wing (zone-in area): Claws of Veeshan faction — non-hostile to CoV allies, dangerous to others
- East Wing (Halls of Testing): Aggressive dragons regardless of faction
- North Wing: Most restricted/dangerous; houses Vulak`Aerr (the top ToV boss)

**Claws of Veeshan faction** is required for the Halls of Testing quest chain. Raising CoV faction requires killing Kromzek giants and Coldain dwarves — which conflicts with Kael and Coldain faction tracks.

### Skyshrine Armor Quest Chain (CoV Faction Path)
**Faction required:** Ally with Claws of Veeshan
**Mold source:** Temple of Veeshan — East Wing (Halls of Testing area)
**Key named:** Dozekar the Cursed and named drakes in East Wing
**Gem requirements:** Same as Kael/Thurgadin armor but turn-in NPCs are in Skyshrine
**Reward:** Full set of Skyshrine-branded armor per class
**Pain score:** YELLOW–RED — East Wing drakes and Dozekar are raid-tier; unpredictable drop rates require multiple kills per slot

### Halls of Testing Quest Chain
**Faction required:** Ally Claws of Veeshan
**Location:** Temple of Veeshan — East Wing
**Key mobs:** Dozekar the Cursed (named drake), other named drakes
**Turn-in NPCs:** Gozzrem, Lendiniara the Keeper, Telkorenar (all West Wing)

**Quest rewards (sampling):**
- Boots of Deep Thought / Boots of the Silent Striding (Gozzrem)
- Buckler of Insight, Mask of the Silver Eyes, Silver Bracelet of Speed (Lendiniara)
- Cloak of the Fire Storm, Pauldrons of the Deep Flame (Telkorenar)

**Item quality:** Best loot in Velious; roughly comparable to or exceeding Kael/Skyshrine tier armor. Not quite epic quality but fills pre-epic gaps.

**Pain score:** RED — Dozekar the Cursed and East Wing named drakes are raid encounters. Unpredictable drops make this a sustained farming commitment even for a full raid.

### Temple of Veeshan Key (ToV Access Key — Clarification)
From research, ToV itself has no key for initial entry. However, multiple sources state that killing King Tormax, Lord Yelinak, or Dain Frostreaver IV grants a "Key of Veeshan" or "ToV key" item used for something inside ToV or in a related quest. This may refer to the Kael Armor quest mold access rather than zone entry itself. The zone entry at Western Wastes is unkeyed (level 46+ only).

**Recommendation for architect:** Verify via database whether any NPC in the live server data references "Key of Veeshan" as a required item for ToV-internal progression (not entry). The P99 wiki says no key for entry; other sources mention kills granting a key. This may be a server-configuration variable.

## Section 4 — Sleeper's Tomb Key Quest

**Quest name:** Key to Sleeper's Tomb
**Quest-giver NPC:** Jaled Dar's shade (Dragon Necropolis, coordinates +1590, -120)
**Purpose:** Access to Kerafyrm's Lair (Sleeper's Tomb) — required to participate in the Sleeper awakening event
**Era:** Velious

### Step-by-step
- Obtain **one talisman** from any of the five First Brood dragons:

| Talisman | Dragon | Zone |
|----------|--------|------|
| Sontalak's Talisman | Sontalak | Western Wastes |
| Lendiniara's Talisman | Lendiniara the Keeper | Temple of Veeshan (West Wing) |
| Klandicar's Talisman | Klandicar | Western Wastes |
| Yelinak's Talisman | Lord Yelinak | Skyshrine |
| Zlandicar's Talisman | Zlandicar | Dragon Necropolis |

**Alternative:** Shard of Hsagra's Talisman — drops from Derakor the Vindicator, King Tormax, The Statue of Rallos Zek (all Kael), or Velketor the Sorcerer (Velketor's Labyrinth). Multiple shards combine into a full talisman.

- Give talisman to Jaled Dar's shade → receive Key to Sleeper's Tomb

### Difficulty Assessment
| Path | Zone | Raid Required? | Pain Score |
|------|------|---------------|------------|
| Sontalak path | Western Wastes | YES — Sontalak ~300k HP | RED |
| Lendiniara path | Temple of Veeshan | YES — deep ToV, needs CoV faction | RED |
| Klandicar path | Western Wastes | YES — Klandicar ~200k HP | RED |
| Yelinak path | Skyshrine | YES — Lord Yelinak full raid | RED |
| Zlandicar path | Dragon Necropolis | YES — Zlandicar ~200k HP | RED |
| Shard path (Kael) | Kael Drakkel | YES — Derakor/Tormax/Statue | RED |

**All paths to the Sleeper's Tomb key require killing at least one raid-tier boss.** There is no non-raid path to Sleeper's Tomb. The zone itself (Kerafyrm's Lair) is a top-tier Velious endgame area.

**Lore note:** Awakening the Sleeper (killing all four Warders inside Sleeper's Tomb) releases Kerafyrm, who rampages through the Icy Wastes and into Skyshrine. This is a one-time server event — once done, it cannot be undone and changes the server state permanently. On a small-group private server, this decision should be deliberate.

## Section 5 — Epic 1.0 Velious-Phase Steps

**Finding:** The Epic 1.0 quests for all 14 classes have NO quest steps that require kills in Velious zones.

All 14 Epic 1.0 chains are entirely Classic + Kunark zones. Velious content does not gate any class epic.

**Implication for audit:** The Velious quest chain section of the audit is about Velious-specific quest progression (Coldain Rings, ToV/Kael/ST access, Halls of Testing), not epic crossovers. There are no epic NPCs in Velious to flag as scaling targets for the quest-chain section.
