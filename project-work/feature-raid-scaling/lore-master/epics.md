## Pain Score Legend
- **GREEN** — completable by 1 player + 5 companions; no structural blockers
- **YELLOW** — at least one step that is noticeably hard (long camp, KoS faction gating, hard raid encounter)
- **RED** — at least one step that is structurally broken for small group (death-touch mechanics, impossible rare spawns, overwhelming multi-raid-group requirement)

## Notes on Era Span
All 14 epics were introduced post-Kunark and span multiple eras. Classic-phase steps are noted where relevant, but most chains require travel across Classic, Kunark, and some Velious content.

## BARD — Singing Short Sword

**Quest start:** Baldric Slezaf (Dreadlands, pyramid near wizard portal)
**Final turn-in:** Baldric Slezaf (same NPC, Dreadlands)
**Era span:** Classic, Kunark

### Progression Steps

1. **Page 24 Top** — Running relay quest: Konia Swiftfoot (W. Karana) → Fajio Knejo (Misty Thicket) → Andad Filla (S. Ro) → Misty Tekchita (Lake Rathetear) → return to Konia. Purely travel, no combat.
2. **Page 24 Bottom** — Collect Mechanical Doll (Marfen Binkdirple, SolA). Turn in at Serra (Estate of Unrest). Then give note to Baenar Swiftsong (S. Karana). Give note to **Maligar** (W. Karana) — he's KoS, must be charmed. Doppelganger spawns (level 45), kill it. Loot head, return to Baenar for Mahlin's Mystical Bongos. Turn in to Konia Swiftfoot.
3. **Page 25** — Three rare drops for Kelkim Menkia (S. Karana):
   - Onyx Drake Gut: **Blackwing** (level 36 drake, Rathe Mountains)
   - Red Wurm Gut: **Nezekezena** (level 51) or **Phurzikon** (level 47), Burning Woods
   - Chromodrac Gut: **Eldrig the Old** (level 51), Skyfire Mountains — rare spawn
4. **Mystical Lute Head** — Kill **Quag Maelstrom** (level 45, OoT seafury island) for Alluring Horn. Then collect from Forpar Fizfla (Steamfont): **Kedge Backbone** (Phinigel Autropos, Kedge Keep, 12hr respawn), **Amygdalan Tendril** (Amygdalan Warriors, Plane of Fear), **Petrified Werewolf Skull** (Drolvarg Warlord, Karnor's Castle).
5. **Mystical Lute Body** — **Red Dragon Scales**: Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair) or Talendor/Zordakalicus Ragefire (Skyfire). **White Dragon Scales**: Lady Vox (Permafrost) or Gorenaire (Dreadlands). Metal Bits (tradeskill). Give all to Forpar.
6. **Undead Dragongut Strings** — Find An Undead Bard (Old Sebilis). Give Mystical Lute Body. **An Undead Bard** attacks, kill it. **Triggered Trakanon** spawns — kill it for Undead Dragongut Strings.
7. Combine all lute parts at Forpar → Mystical Lute.
8. **Final turn-in:** Baldric Slezaf receives all 4 components → Singing Short Sword.

### Raid Encounters
| Encounter | Zone | Level | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|-------|
| Maligar Doppelganger | W. Karana | 45 | Spawned; manageable with group |
| Blackwing | Rathe Mountains | 36 | Small group friendly |
| Nezekezena / Phurzikon | Burning Woods | 47–51 | Rare spawns, 90s cycle |
| Eldrig the Old | Skyfire Mountains | 51 | Rare spawn, two spawn points |
| Quag Maelstrom | Ocean of Tears | 45 | Seafury cyclops island |
| Phinigel Autropos | Kedge Keep | 50 | 12-hour respawn |
| Amygdalan Warriors | Plane of Fear | 48–52 | Need PoFear access |
| Drolvarg Warlord | Karnor's Castle | 51–54 | Rare drop |
| Lord Nagafen | Nagafen's Lair | 55 | Full raid in-era |
| Lady Vox | Permafrost | 55 | Full raid in-era |
| Gorenaire | Dreadlands | ~55 | Outdoor dragon, raid |
| Triggered Trakanon | Old Sebilis | High | See note below |

**Trakanon note:** The triggered Trakanon in the Undead Bard encounter is a scripted, private spawn — not the zone boss. It still hits like Trakanon (level unknown, massive damage). This is one of the hardest steps on the bard epic.

### Non-Encounter Blockers
- Maligar is permanently KoS — requires Charm spell or sneak/FD approach
- Phinigel Autropos: 12-hour respawn, uncommon drop on Kedge Backbone
- Eldrig the Old: rare spawn in Skyfire (two spawn points), low cycle time but competes with other spawns

### Cross-Chain Gating
None. Bard epic is self-contained.

### Small-Group Pain Score: **RED**
Triggered Trakanon in Old Sebilis is a structural problem — it's a private encounter but still hits like a raid boss. Lord Nagafen and Lady Vox also require what were full raid forces in era. Phinigel Autropos requires repeated visits if the uncommon drop doesn't land. Total chain length is very long with numerous zones.

## CLERIC — Water Sprinkler of Nem Ankh

**Quest start:** Shmendrik Lavawalker (Lake Rathetear)
**Final turn-in:** Avatar of Water (Timorous Deep shore, near Omat Vastsea)
**Era span:** Classic, Kunark

### Progression Steps

1. **Orb of Frozen Water:**
   - Kill Lord Bergurgle (Lake Rathetear, underwater cave) — rare spawn, 29-min timer. Loot crown.
   - Give crown to Shmendrik (3hr respawn) → Oil of Fennin Ro. Natasha Whitewater spawns, kills Shmendrik. Kill spawned Spirit of Flame (level 40). Loot Damaged Goblin Crown, give to Natasha for Ornate Sea Shell.
   - Give shell to Omat Vastsea (Timorous Deep oasis island) → Coral Statue of Tarew.
   - Give statue to A Seeker (Temple of SolRo, daytime only, male version only) → spawns Plasmatic Priest (level 55, 13K HP, Complete Heal, AE Tremor). Kill for robe.
   - Kill Lord Gimblox (SolA, rare spawn, 18-min timer) for signet ring.
   - Give robe to Omat → Orb of Frozen Water. Give ring to Natasha → Ornate Sea Shell (v2).
2. **Orb of Clear Water:**
   - Give shell to Naxot Deepwater (Burning Woods, nighttime only) → spawns **Ixiblat Fer** (level 62, 32K+ HP, 575 damage/hit, 300 AE fire). Kill for sceptre.
   - Kill **Overking Bathezid** (Chardok royals, level 63, 500 AE, CH, Gravity Flux, ~2-group requirement). Requires picklock to access. Loot Singed Scroll.
   - Give sceptre + scroll to Omat → Orb of Clear Water. Give message to Natasha → Shimmering Pearl.
3. **Orb of Vapor:**
   - Give pearl to Zordak Ragefire (SolB) → spawns Zordakalicus Ragefire (dragon-humanoid, clone of Nagafen). Kill for Heart.
   - Give heart to Omat → Orb of Vapor. Jhassad Oceanson spawns nearby.
4. **Final:** Give all 3 orbs to Jhassad → Orb of Triumvirate. Avatar of Water spawns. Give orb to Avatar → Water Sprinkler of Nem Ankh.

### Raid Encounters
| Encounter | Zone | Level | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|-------|
| Plasmatic Priest | Temple of SolRo | 55 | 13K HP, triggered by turn-in |
| Ixiblat Fer | Burning Woods | 62 | 32K+ HP, 575 dmg, AE fire; spawned |
| Overking Bathezid | Chardok | 63 | ~2 good groups in-era |
| Zordakalicus Ragefire | Nagafen's Lair | 55 | Dragon, Nagafen clone; spawned |

### Non-Encounter Blockers
- Shmendrik Lavawalker: 3-hour respawn — if you miss the Natasha window, you wait
- Naxot Deepwater: spawns nighttime only in Burning Woods
- A Seeker: daytime only in Temple of SolRo; must be male version (female spawns at night)
- Multiple Natasha Whitewater despawn timers (5 minutes) — tight timing required throughout

### Cross-Chain Gating
None.

### Small-Group Pain Score: **RED**
Ixiblat Fer at level 62 with 32K HP and 575/hit was considered multi-raid-group content in era. Overking Bathezid (level 63) is similarly brutal. These two encounters alone gate the entire chain. Tight NPC timing windows add frustration on top.

## DRUID — Nature Walker's Scimitar

**Quest start:** Telin Darkforest (Burning Woods, requires Amiable faction)
**Final turn-in:** Xanuusus (North Karana)
**Era span:** Classic, Kunark (heavy Kunark content)

### Progression Steps

1. Worn Note from Telin Darkforest (Burning Woods, Amiable faction required — may need "Muffin for Pandos" quest first).
2. Ring from Faelin Bloodbriar (Greater Faydark, orc pawn PH, 2.5-min respawn) → coin from Giz X'Tin (Kithicor, roaming) → return to Telin.
3. East Karana 5-part chain: Althele → Sionae → Nuien → Teloa → spawn of Dark Elf Corruptor + 2 Reavers → loot Fleshbound Tome → return for Earth Stained Note.
4. Forage quest: Shiny Tin Bowl from Ella Foodcrafter (Misty Thicket). Forage 4 items (Chilled Tundra Root, Ripened Heartfruit, Speckled Molded Mushroom, Sweetened Mudroot). Combine.
5. **Keepers of the Art faction:** 3,000 Batwing turn-ins to Niola Impholder (S. Felwithe) to reach Amiable → interact with Alrik Farsight (Timorous Deep).
6. Running chains through Felwithe (Farios Elianos ↔ Merchant Nora ↔ Alrik Farsight).
7. Rose of Firiona forage + trade chain through Surefall Glade and Frontier Mountains skeleton.
8. Jade Reaver from City of Mist black reavers (rare, behind locked door) → Enchanted Clay from Kinlo Strongarm (N. Kaladim).
9. Pottery combine → Softly Glowing Stone from Ella Foodcrafter.
10. **Karnor's Castle:** Kill Venril Sathir. Wait ~20 hours for remains to spawn. Give Firefly Globe (night-only summon spell, Dance of Fireflies) to remains → Spirit of Venril Sathir spawns. Give Resurrection scroll (level 47 cleric) → Venril Sathir spawns. Kill for Pulsing Green Stone.
11. Give stone to Foloal Stormforest (Firiona Vie) → Warmly Glowing Stone → give to Ella → Elaborate Scimitar.
12. **Cleansed Spirit of Antonica:** 3 tainted mob camps (aquagoblin in Lake Rathetear, hill giant in Rathe Mountains, wooly mammoth in Everfrost). Give drops to withered treant (N. Karana) → Warm Pulsing Treant Heart → Yeka Ias (S. Karana) → Cleansed Spirit of Antonica.
13. **Cleansed Spirit of Faydwer:** 3 tainted mob camps (seafury cyclops OoT, seahorse Kedge Keep, brownie Lesser Faydark). Give to pained unicorn (Lesser Faydark) → Silox Azrix (Ak'Anon) → Cleansed Spirit of Faydwer.
14. **Cleansed Spirit of Kunark:** Kill corrupted gorilla (Emerald Jungle), corrupted barracuda (Lake of Ill Omen — potential multi-hour camp), **Ulump Pujluk** (level 55, Swamp of No Hope, ~2hr respawn, group of 60s recommended). Kill **weak Faydedar** (Timorous Deep, spawned by Dolgin Codslayer, ~12hr respawn). Give drops to Nekexin Virulence (Overthere) → Cleansed Spirit of Kunark.
15. **Final:** Give all 3 spirits + Elaborate Scimitar to Xanuusus (N. Karana) → Nature Walker's Scimitar.

### Raid Encounters
| Encounter | Zone | Level | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|-------|
| Dark Elf Corruptor + 2 Reavers | East Karana | ~40s | Spawned by Teloa event |
| Venril Sathir (triggered) | Karnor's Castle | 55 | 20hr wait for remains; requires Firefly Globe (night-only) + Rez scroll |
| Ulump Pujluk | Swamp of No Hope | 55 | ~2hr respawn, summons; group of 60s recommended |
| Weak Faydedar | Timorous Deep | ~55 | Spawned by Dolgin (12hr respawn); group of 60s recommended |

### Non-Encounter Blockers
- **Keepers of the Art faction:** Must reach Amiable minimum (3,000 Batwing turn-ins). City of Mist black reaver camp also requires picklock class.
- **Telin Darkforest faction:** Requires Amiable (Muffin for Pandos quest if not already met).
- **Firefly Globe:** Only summoned at night via Dance of Fireflies — if you don't have a Druid/Ranger with the spell, you're stuck.
- **Corrupted barracuda (Lake of Ill Omen):** 8 PH locations, potentially multi-hour camp.
- **Dolgin Codslayer:** ~12-hour respawn in Timorous Deep.
- **20-hour wait** after killing Venril Sathir for remains to spawn.

### Cross-Chain Gating
Druid and Ranger epics share the exact same Cleansed Spirit of Antonica/Faydwer/Kunark sub-chains (steps 12–14) and many of the same NPCs (Telin Darkforest, Faelin Bloodbriar, Giz X'Tin, Ella Foodcrafter, Xanuusus). If doing both on the same server, the tainted mob camps can be shared.

### Small-Group Pain Score: **RED**
This is the longest epic in the game by number of zones and steps. Venril Sathir requires the 20-hour waiting mechanic AND a night-only component. Faydedar has a 12-hour respawn. The tainted mob camps are multi-hour affairs each. The Keepers of the Art faction grind is significant. Three separate "Cleansed Spirit" sub-chains each with multiple tainted mob camps.

## ENCHANTER — Staff of the Serpent

**Quest start:** Reania Jukle (Qeynos Catacombs) for Jeb's Seal chain; pieces can be done in any order
**Final turn-in:** A sarnak imitator (Burning Woods, loc -1200, -4000)
**Era span:** Classic, Kunark

### Progression Steps

**Jeb's Seal (prerequisite):**
- Empty Ink Vial from Reania Jukle (Qeynos Catacombs) → charm a ghoul scribe (Lower Guk) to get Ink of the Dark.
- Shining Metallic Robes from ghoul arch magus (Lower Guk rare) or Yendar Starpyre (Steamfont, always up, level 65).
- Give robes to Rilgor Plegnog (Ak'Anon) → Mechanical Pen.
- Buy Quill + Parchment from Qeynos vendor. Give to Chrislin Baker (W. Karana) → spawns Thrackin Griften (level 55, 20K HP). Kill for White Paper.
- Give Ink, Pen, Paper to Stofo Olan (Erudin inn) → Copy of Notes → give to sarnak imitator (Burning Woods) → Jeb's Seal.

**1st Piece of Staff:**
- Xolion Rod: Kill Vessel Drozlin (East Cabilis sewers, level 60, 18K HP, 9hr respawn, levitate required).
- Innoruuk's Word: Kill Verina Tomb (Neriak Third Gate, 13hr respawn).
- Head of a Prince: Kill Prince Selrach Di'Zok (Chardok, level 61, 2hr respawn, picklock required) → give head to Joren Nobleheart (S. Felwithe) → Chalice of Kings.
- Snow Blossoms: Ground spawn in Oggok → NPC chain → Snow Blossoms.
- Combine all 4 in Enchanters Sack, give to Modani Qu'Loni (Overthere) → 1st Piece.

**2nd Piece of Staff:**
- Spoon: Kill Cazel (S. Ro, roaming, always drops).
- The One Key: Ground spawn in Overthere (levitate required, 5hr respawn).
- Lost Scroll: Ground spawn in Dalnir (5hr respawn).
- Charm and Sacrifice: **Plane of Sky** Quest Island (5hr respawn).
- Combine, give to Clockwork VIIX (Ak'Anon) → 2nd Piece.

**3rd Piece of Staff:**
- All require charming rare/named NPCs and giving dull gems to charmed targets:
  - Enchanted Diamond: charm Spectral Librarian (Kaesora)
  - Enchanted Sapphire: charm Felia Goldwing (Skyfire, rare spawn)
  - Enchanted Emerald: charm Impaler Tzilug (Overthere, 24hr respawn)
  - Enchanted Ruby: charm Wraith of Jaxion (City of Mist, picklock required)
- Combine, give to Nadia Starfeast (Firiona Vie) → 3rd Piece.

**4th Piece of Staff:**
- Head of the Serpent: Kill **Wraith of a Shissar** (**Plane of Fear**, 9hr respawn).
- Essence of a Vampire: Kill forsaken revenant (**Plane of Hate**, rare drop).
- Sands of the Mystics: Kill **The Tangrin** (Field of Bone, level 54, 32K HP, 12hr respawn).
- Essence of a Ghost: Kill **Ghost of Kindle** (**The Hole**, level 50).
- Combine, give to Polzin Mrid (The Hole) → 4th Piece. Combine all 4 pieces → Bundle of Staves → sarnak imitator → **Staff of the Serpent**.

### Raid Encounters
| Encounter | Zone | Level | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|-------|
| Thrackin Griften | W. Karana | 55 | 20K HP; spawned |
| Vessel Drozlin | East Cabilis | 60 | 18K HP, 9hr respawn, levitate required |
| Prince Selrach Di'Zok | Chardok | 61 | Picklock required |
| Wraith of a Shissar | Plane of Fear | — | 9hr respawn; PoFear access required |
| Forsaken Revenant | Plane of Hate | — | Rare drop; PoHate access required |
| The Tangrin | Field of Bone | 54 | 32K HP, 12hr respawn |
| Ghost of Kindle | The Hole | 50 | |

### Non-Encounter Blockers
- **Charm requirement throughout 3rd piece:** Enchanter must be able to charm level-appropriate mobs; charmed targets are the turn-in NPCs — if charm breaks or NPC dies, you lose the gem item.
- **Felia Goldwing (Skyfire):** Rare spawn; tracker recommended.
- **Impaler Tzilug (Overthere):** 24-hour respawn — one shot per day.
- **Plane of Sky access:** Required for 2nd piece ground spawn.

### Cross-Chain Gating
None.

### Small-Group Pain Score: **YELLOW**
Most pieces are manageable for a solo enchanter with companions. The charm-based 3rd piece steps are unique — they require active enchanter participation (can't be auto-completed by companions). Plane of Fear and Hate access add logistical overhead but those zones are accessible. The 24-hour Impaler Tzilug timer is the worst single blocker.

## MAGICIAN — Orb of Mastery

**Quest start:** Walnan (Butcherblock), Jahsohn Aksot (Commonlands), Akkstaff (Najena), Rykas (Lake Rathetear)
**Final turn-in:** Master of Elements (Plane of Sky Quest Island)
**Era span:** Classic, Kunark

### Progression Steps

**Power of the Elements:** Drop farm across Castle Mistmoore, SolA, Lesser Faydark, Kaesora → turn in to Walnan.

**Words of Mastery:** Rare drops from magicians in Najena → Akkstaff.

**Words of Magi'Kot:** Drops from Kithicor dread wolves (night only), Unrest tentacle terrors, Lower Guk bloodthirsty ghouls → Jahsohn Aksot.

**Power of the Orb:** Combine all three words/powers at Rykas (Lake Rathetear).

**Element of Fire:**
- Burning Embers: Nezekezena/Phurzikon/Gylton (Burning Woods)
- Blazing Wand: **Undertow** (Kedge Keep rare, 6hr respawn)
- Torch of the Elements: **Neh'Ashirr** (City of Mist, level 55+, charm/mana drain required, 30–90min respawn)
- Turn in to **Jennus Lyklobar** (Skyfire Mountains, roaming gnome, ~48hr spawn after server up)

**Element of Wind:**
- Crown of Elemental Mastery: **Plane of Sky Island 7** mobs (tradable)
- **Pegasus Feather Cloak:** Kill **Quillmane** (S. Karana) — rare spawn, tracker required, potentially the longest camp of any epic
- Elemental Binder: The Hole (common, tradable)
- Turn in to Kihun Solstin (Plane of Sky Quest Island)

**Element of Earth:**
- **Staff of Elemental Mastery: Earth:** Kill **Magi P'Tasa** (**Plane of Hate**)
- Dirt of Underfoot: Slixin Klex (Burning Woods, always up)
- Shovel of Ponz + Broom of Trilon: Sub-quests via Vira (Temple of SolRo) requiring Najena drops + vendor gems
- Turn in to Tiblner Milnik (Firiona Vie)

**Element of Water:**
- Rain of Karana: Tarbul Earthstrider (E. Karana, always drops)
- **Staff of Elemental Mastery: Water:** Kill **Phinigel Autropos** (Kedge Keep, 12hr respawn)
- Tears of Erollisi: Captain Rottgrime (Overthere, always drops, requires CC)
- Turn in to Jinalis Andir (Dagnor's Cauldron)

**Final:** Give all 4 Elements to Master of Elements (Plane of Sky Quest Island) → Spell: Summon Orb.

### Raid Encounters
| Encounter | Zone | Level | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|-------|
| Neh'Ashirr | City of Mist | 55 | Casts Complete Heal + Charm; mana drain recommended |
| Magi P'Tasa | Plane of Hate | — | PoHate raid content |
| Phinigel Autropos | Kedge Keep | 50 | 12-hour respawn |
| Undertow | Kedge Keep | — | Rare spawn, 6hr respawn |

### Non-Encounter Blockers
- **Quillmane (S. Karana):** Extremely rare PH-based spawn; tracker almost mandatory; described as potentially the longest camp in any epic. Only drops Pegasus Feather Cloak, no other loot.
- **Jennus Lyklobar (Skyfire):** ~48-hour spawn timer after server comes up; roaming. If killed or missed, very long wait.
- **Crown of Elemental Mastery:** Requires Plane of Sky Island 7 access (deep into PoSky keying chain).
- Najena drop rate for torn pages can be slow.

### Cross-Chain Gating
- Plane of Sky access is required for both Element of Wind (Island 7 Crown) and the final turn-in (Quest Island). Reaching Island 7 requires clearing through Island 6 bosses including death-touch encounters.

### Small-Group Pain Score: **RED**
Quillmane is a notorious server-wide bottleneck — a single rare spawn with one relevant drop and no other purpose. Island 7 for Crown of Elemental Mastery requires clearing through multiple death-touch bosses in PoSky (Keeper of Souls on 4, Spiroc Lord on 5, Queen Bee on 6). Plane of Hate for Magi P'Tasa requires raid-level access. Multiple 12-hour+ respawn timers.

## MONK — Celestial Fists

**Quest start:** Various (Skyfire for book, Erudin for Danl, Timorous Deep for Lheao)
**Final turn-in:** Real Kaiaren (Trakanon's Teeth)
**Era span:** Classic, Kunark

### Progression Steps

**Robe of the Whistling Fists (choose path):**
- **Option A (direct drop):** Kill Brother Zephyl (Rathe Mountains, level 61, 32K HP, 300s/hit, 5-min PH) or Brother Qwinn (S. Karana) for Robe of the Lost Circle (~20–40% drop).
  - Faction consequence: -500 Ashen Order per kill. Recover via orc kills.
- **Option B (quest):** Requires Code of Zan Fi (Targin the Rock, SolB, 15% spawn) + Idol (Raster of Guk, Lower Guk, 10% spawn) + Red Sash of Order + Purple Headband chains. Very complex.
- Collect Metal Pipe (Zan) from drolvarg pawbuster (Karnor's Castle) + Metal Pipe (Fi) from iksar betrayer (Chardok, always drops).
- Turn in Robe + Pipes to Brother Balatin (Dreadlands, KoS — charm or sneak required) → Robe of the Whistling Fists.

**Celestial Fists book:**
- Kill named mobs in Skyfire for Immortals (uncommon drop from named wyverns/drakes/wurms).
- Turn in to Tomekeeper Danl (Erudin, 2nd floor library) → Danl's Reference.
- Give Danl's Reference + Robe of Whistling Fists to Lheao (Timorous Deep) → Celestial Fists (book). WARNING: This consumes the Robe permanently.

**Demon Fangs (elemental master chain):**
- Challenge Eejag (Lavastorm, say "i challenge eejag" to any fire sprite) — level 55, soloable.
- Give Charred Scale to a presence on Island 1.5 (Plane of Sky) → kill Gwan (1 group).
- Give Breath of Gwan to sleeping ogre (Nurga) → kill Trunt (trivial).
- Give Trunt's Head to Astral Projection (Overthere) → Eye of Kaiaren.
- Give Eye to Astral Projection (Lake of Ill Omen, levitate required) → Vorash + Deep spawn. Kill both, then **Xenevorash** spawns (32K HP, 500/hit, 12-second PBAE stun "Fist of Sentience"). Kill for Demon Fangs. Need 2 groups in-era.

**Final:**
- Give book to "Crazy" Kaiaren (Trakanon's Teeth, SE side near Old Sebilis) — he attacks, run away. This spawns "Real" Kaiaren (small hut, +300 +2450).
- Give book alone to Real Kaiaren → Book of Celestial Fists.
- Give book + Demon Fangs to Real Kaiaren → **Celestial Fists**.
- WARNING: Do not complete this turn-in without Demon Fangs in hand — Real Kaiaren despawns in ~30 minutes.

### Raid Encounters
| Encounter | Zone | Level | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|-------|
| Brother Zephyl / Qwinn | Rathe/S. Karana | 61 | 32K HP, 300s/hit; 1000 unresistable ranged hit every 30s; rare drop |
| Eejag | Lavastorm | 55 | Soloable at appropriate level |
| Gwan | Plane of Sky 1.5 | — | 1 group |
| Xenevorash | Lake of Ill Omen | — | 32K HP, 500/hit, PBAE 12s stun; 2 groups in-era |

### Non-Encounter Blockers
- **Brother Zephyl/Qwinn:** ~20–40% drop rate on Robe of Lost Circle. With -500 Ashen Order faction per kill, repeated kills must be compensated with orc grinding. Faction recovery is genuinely time-consuming.
- **Raster of Guk:** 10% spawn from PH in Lower Guk. If taking quest path, this can be days of camping.
- **Plane of Sky Island 1.5 access:** Requires Key of Swords (Miniature Sword from Island 1 trash after killing Thunder Spirit Princess).
- **Lheao turn-in:** Consumes Robe of Whistling Fists permanently — can't redo if you make a mistake.

### Cross-Chain Gating
Plane of Sky Island 1.5 access required (minor keying — just Island 1 boss + Miniature Sword).

### Small-Group Pain Score: **YELLOW**
The Brother camps are harsh but manageable with companions. The 10% Raster spawn is brutal if taking quest path. Xenevorash is the hardest single encounter — 32K HP with a 12-second PBAE stun means your whole group gets locked down repeatedly. The Kaiaren final turn-in timing window is a failure point if you aren't prepared.

## NECROMANCER — Scythe of the Shadowed Soul

**Quest start:** Sir Edwin Motte kill (Rathe Mountains/various)
**Final turn-in:** Kazen Fecae (Lake Rathetear)
**Era span:** Classic, Kunark

### Progression Steps

1. Kill Sir Edwin Motte (Rathe Mountains inn area, rare spawn) → Head. Give to Kazen Fecae (Lake Rathetear) → Symbol of the Apprentice.
2. Get Flowing Black Robe (from named in Najena or Overthere; tradable). Give to Venenzi Oberzendi (Nektulos Forest) with Symbol → Twisted Symbol + Rolling Stone Moss.
3. Give both to Emkel Kabae (Lake Rathetear SE island) → Symbol of the Serpent.
4. Kill **Grand Herbalist Mak'ha** (Chardok herbalist room, rare spawn) for Manisi Herb (rare drop).
5. Give Symbol of Serpent to Ssessthrass (Swamp of No Hope) → Scaled Symbol. Give Scaled Symbol + Manisi Herb → Refined Manisi Herb.
6. Give Refined Manisi Herb to Emkel → Symbol of Testing. **Combat event:** Give Symbol of Testing to Kazen → 3 sequential mobs spawn: Bone Golem → Failed Apprentice → **Tortured Soul** (drops Symbol of Insanity).
7. **Plane of Sky:** Give items to Drakis Bloodcaster (Quest Room) — need Black Silk Cape (Keeper of Souls Island 4, uncommon), Silver Disc (Azarack Island 2), Spiroc Feathers (Island 3). Get Shadowy Thoughts → Jzil G'Six spawns → give 3 items for Cloak of Spiroc Feathers.
8. Get **Slime Blood of Cazic-Thule** (Plane of Fear — from Cazic-Thule or Terror/Dread/Fright; tradable) and **Eye of Innoruuk** (Plane of Hate mini-named).
9. Give Symbol of Insanity + Journal of Drendico (from Drendico Metalbones, Timorous Deep) + Slime Blood + Eye of Innoruuk + Cloak → Prepared Reagent Box.

## Small-Group Pain Score: **RED** (Islands 4+)
Islands 1–3: **GREEN** — no death touch, manageable fights.
Island 4 (Keeper of Souls): **YELLOW/RED** — death touch every 30s requires tank rotation. Single tank with companions may be viable with fast companion cycling.
Island 5 (Spiroc Lord): **RED** — death touch + long stuns + 350/hit. Most structurally difficult encounter in Sky.
Islands 6–8: **RED** — all involve death touch; Island 6 Queen Bee possibly hardest in zone.

## Notes for Small-Group Design
- Death touch mechanics are the core problem. In vanilla EQ, death touch was instant-kill regardless of HP/AC. If the server's scaling pass can modify death touch damage (or convert it to a very high but survivable hit), Islands 4+ become manageable for a small group.
- The key chain is sequential and gated by boss kills — cannot skip. A group that can't clear Island 4 cannot reach Islands 5–8 where many epic items drop.
