# Universal Summon Corpse Spell — Feature Brief

## What
A free, level 1, class-flavored *Summon Corpse* spell scribed by all 12 casting-capable classes. Solves the "corpse stuck deep in a dungeon" problem for solo-with-companions play, since hiring a Necromancer isn't an option.

## Why
Corpse runs are the harshest punishment in classic EQ, and the player has no access to other players' Necromancers. The companion system can't reliably help retrieve a corpse from inside a dangerous zone. This spell gives every caster a built-in safety valve without trivializing combat or wiping the death penalty entirely (XP loss, equipment unequipped, still need to be in the zone).

## Mechanics
- **Level:** 1, all 12 casting-capable classes (CLR, DRU, SHM, NEC, WIZ, MAG, ENC, PAL, SHD, RNG, BST, BRD)
- **Mana cost:** 0
- **Reagents:** none
- **Cast time:** 6 seconds
- **Range/target:** Self; same zone as corpse; corpse appears at caster's feet
- **Cooldown:** 3 minutes
- **Effect:** Identical to existing summon corpse mechanic — pulls the player's own corpse to the caster's location

## Acquisition
- **New characters:** Sold as scrolls on standard class spell vendors in starting cities (matches normal level 1 spell shopping)
- **Existing characters:** Auto-scribed silently in the next server start after the patch (no MOTD)

## Out of scope (for this feature)
- Summoning other players' / companions' corpses
- Cross-zone summons
- Anything that affects XP loss, equipment-on-corpse, or rez timers
